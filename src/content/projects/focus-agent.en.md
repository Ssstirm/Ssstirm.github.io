## What it is

Focus Agent is a system that runs a focus-group session entirely with LLM-driven virtual participants. Each participant carries a persona, a reasoning style, and a memory of the conversation, and the moderator steers the group toward an interview goal — exactly as a human moderator would.

## Why

Real focus groups are slow, expensive, and hard to iterate on. Researchers and product teams often need to pressure-test a concept long before they can recruit and run a full session. Focus Agent is a fast loop: you draft a study, run it against a panel of synthetic participants, watch where the conversation goes off the rails, and revise.

## Result

Published at the 24th ACM International Conference on Intelligent Virtual Agents (IVA 2024) as first author.
